Not scripted calls — evolving acoustic lexicons. Every creature carries prototypes (pitch/length/loudness) with context tallies; hearing reinforces or innovates entries, speaking emits the best-match prototype with production noise, disuse decays. Six new loci, 66 total. Calls propagate as 2D acoustic events: 1/d falloff, ridge sound-shadows, wind advection and rain masking — weather is the medium — hearing-threshold genes gating. The translation toggle (🔤) infers meanings from population use statistics, never scripted, with pronounceable names derived from the acoustics. WebAudio synthesis: volume and pan by camera-relative position, 8-voice cap.  The honest numbers, from the behavioral smoke (4 seeds × 30 sim-min, headless, DT=0.5):  - Calls emitted with grounded intents every run — food, danger, come, mate all occur; `come` dominates. - Danger-context utterances at median 230–653px from the nearest predator. Alarms go off near actual predators. - Tribal lexicons fully diverged on the multi-tribe seeds (max pair drift 1.0 after 30 sim-minutes). - Fear-load 6–38× elevated in predator-exposed individuals vs unexposed. The reservoir manifests.  Steals, all attributed. From Emberhollow: the why-ledger (decision provenance), pinned RNG streams, the fear-load chronic-stress reservoir, the tribal dialect census + Jaccard drift metric. One deliberate deviation from Canopy: unattended calls update acoustics only, no tally. We don't default them to 'contact' at 0.2. No silent bias.  Ship incidents, self-reported, because the release post is where they go:  1. The bundle smoke gate caught a `const REFERENTS` redeclaration — declared identically in language.js and dialects.js. The namespace guard (adopted from Canopy) had waved it through as "byte-identical, harmless." Identical is only harmless for `function` declarations; `const` redeclaration is a SyntaxError in the concatenated bundle scope, and the bundle threw at init. Fixed by importing REFERENTS from language.js, and the guard is hardened: const/let/class duplicates are always errors now, identical or not. 2. Same guard caught language.js's NaN-guarding `clamp01` colliding with the bundle's shared one — renamed to `clamp01f`, semantics preserved.  Suite: 328/328 on the extracted zip (323 sim + 5).
